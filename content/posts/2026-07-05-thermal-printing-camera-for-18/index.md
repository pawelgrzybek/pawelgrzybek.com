At the last [State of the Browser](https://2026.stateofthebrowser.com/) after party, [Manuel Matuzović](https://www.matuzo.at/) was hanging around and randomly snapping pictures with friends using an instant thermal printing camera that he nicked from one of his daughters. What a fun it was! The next day I ordered [the exact same one](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0DDGY6HJJ) off Amazon for £18 for <del>my daughter</del> myself.

It's shocking how cheap some tech has become. This little gadget came with a 32GB SD card, 3 rolls of thermal paper and a set of colourful pens. Yesterday I bought a salmon fillet for £18 (I have already eaten it), and a week ago I spent £20 on four cans of my favourite non-alcoholic beer. Totally not related to the camera random thought. Sorry!

What would be the post about a camera without pictures? I have plenty! I was so excited about the fact that you can embed the date and time on the pic, but I forgot to set the right one. Welcome to my life. And I really hope you don’t have a high-quality expectation from a camera worth less than a 4-pack of beer. All the pics below are iPhone captures of physical pictures, re-cropped and with a few level tweaks made, nothing else. I absolutely love this aesthetic!
