## Top picks

### [2027 Web Platform Feature Ranking](https://interop-rank.fxdx.dev/)

Interop is an effort by multiple leading web companies to make the platform more interoperable. Friends at Firefox opened a survey for the second year to check what users need. This is going to help them prioritise the right features but also shape the roadmap for Firefox development. [Ana Rodrigues publiched her picks in the "My Interop feature ranking"](https://ohhelloana.blog/my-interop-2027/), and then [Tyler Sticka folloed here in the "My Interop 2027 Ranking"](https://tylersticka.com/journal/interop-2027-rank/). I also cherry-picked mine… This is your low-effort option to contribute to the shape of the future web, so please take a minute and fill it in.
