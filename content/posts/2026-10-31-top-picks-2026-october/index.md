## Top picks

### [2027 Web Platform Feature Ranking](https://interop-rank.fxdx.dev/)

Interop is an effort by multiple leading web companies to make the platform more interoperable. Friends at Firefox opened a survey for the second year to check what users need. This is going to help them prioritise the right features but also shape the roadmap for Firefox development. [Ana Rodrigues publiched her picks in the "My Interop feature ranking"](https://ohhelloana.blog/my-interop-2027/), and then [Tyler Sticka folloed here in the "My Interop 2027 Ranking"](https://tylersticka.com/journal/interop-2027-rank/). I also cherry-picked mine in the ["My Interop 2027 picks"](/my-interop-2027-picks/). This is your low-effort option to contribute to the shape of the future web, so please take a minute and fill it in.

### [Deno is joining Cloudflare](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare)

This news was a little bit sad to me at first. I invested a big chunk of my life using and trusting Deno's vision, but over the years I noticed the stagnation of this runtime and all the projects surrounding it. I’m not surprised at all by this news. Now I’m happy that good people with the right vision for building good primitives for the web are joining a bigger organisation, where they can focus their efforts on building good things and not worry about money.
